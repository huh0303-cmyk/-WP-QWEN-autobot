South Korea's E-9 quota for 2026 is 80,000 workers under the Employment Permit System (EPS). That is down from 130,000 in 2025 and 165,000 in 2024, so each hiring round matters more for small and mid-sized employers. The cap is a ceiling, not a promise of arrivals: the Ministry of Employment and Labor (MOEL) releases permits in rounds through the year.

What the 80,000 cap is made of

The 2026 plan, approved on 22 December 2025, splits the cap into 70,000 sector quotas and a 10,000 flexible pool that MOEL can draw on when a sector has more demand than its quota covers. Press reports on the plan put manufacturing at 50,000 and agriculture and livestock at 10,000. A temporary shipbuilding quota ended at the close of 2025.

E-9 is only one part of the non-professional workforce plan. Seasonal workers on E-8 visas are planned separately, at 109,000 for 2026.

What is new in the final round

From round 5, employers that complete the group training on human-rights protection and core labor law run by the Korea Human Resources Development Service receive bonus points in the allocation scoring. MOEL's release does not state how many points the bonus is worth.

New workers are allocated by a points-based system. Workplaces that meet the permit requirements are ranked by score; higher-scoring workplaces are served first and the rest receive a waiting number. Applicants are told the result by text message, and it can also be checked on Work24.

Planning ahead for the next hiring cycle

The 2026 plan was set in the second half of December, and the 2025 plan was set in the same month, so the 2027 quota and round calendar are likely to be announced around year-end. Until then, employers can prepare the parts that do not depend on the number:

  • Finish the domestic recruitment effort before the window opens. The 2026 round notices ask for at least 7 days.
  • Have housing paperwork ready. The round 4 notice from a regional labor office asked for a housing facility checklist with the building register or lease and photos.
  • Consider the human-rights and labor-law training, since it now earns bonus points.
  • Read the notice for each round for the exact industry codes, documents and dates.

FAQ

Is 80,000 the number of workers who will arrive in 2026?

No. It is the ceiling for new permits. In 2025, against a 130,000 ceiling, E-9 visa issuances had reached only 48,668 by the end of November, according to the Japan Institute for Labour Policy and Training.

Is there another application window in 2026?

Round 5 is the last one for 2026 and closes on 27 November. Dates for 2027 will be announced by MOEL once the quota is set.

Where do employers apply?

Through the Work24 website or at a regional employment and labor office, after the domestic recruitment period.
