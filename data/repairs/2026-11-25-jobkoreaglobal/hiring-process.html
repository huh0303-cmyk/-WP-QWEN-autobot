To hire E-9 workers, a small or mid-sized Korean employer must first show that it cannot find Korean workers, then go through the Employment Permit System (EPS). The Ministry of Employment and Labor (MOEL) runs it, and the order of steps matters: skipping one, or applying outside a window, means the application is not accepted. This guide follows the process as MOEL described it in its 2026 round notices.

Step 1: Check that your business can hire under E-9

E-9 hiring is open only to certain sectors: manufacturing, construction, agriculture and livestock, services, fisheries, mining and forestry. Eligibility within a sector is set by industry code, and each round's notice says which codes qualify. Logistics is not one of the named sectors, so check your industry code before you plan around E-9 workers.

Step 2: Try to hire Korean workers first

Before applying, employers must make a documented recruitment effort for Korean workers. The 2026 round notices from MOEL ask for at least 7 days; the final-round release repeats this. The requirement has differed by sector and year, so read the period in the notice for the round you are applying to.

Step 3: Apply inside the round's window

Applications are accepted only during a round. For the final 2026 round that was 23-27 November. You can apply on the Work24 website (www.work24.go.kr) or in person at a regional employment and labor office. The round 4 notice from one regional office listed these documents:

  • The permit application form, with the business registration (a farm registration for agriculture).
  • A housing facility checklist, with the building register or lease contract and photos of the housing.

Housing is checked at the application stage, so it has to be arranged before you apply, not after.

Step 4: Wait for the points-based allocation

New workers are allocated by score. Workplaces that meet the requirements are ranked, higher scores are served first, and lower-scoring workplaces get a waiting number. Results are sent by text message and can be checked on Work24; the final 2026 round announced results on 11 December. Starting with that round, employers who complete the human-rights and core labor-law group training run by the Korea Human Resources Development Service earn bonus points.

Step 5: Collect the employment permit

Permits are issued in sector order. In the final round, manufacturing and mining employers could collect on 14-16 December, and construction, agriculture and livestock, fisheries, forestry and services on 17-21 December. If a permit is not issued in your slot, regional offices have asked employers to visit during their sector's period with a copy of the business registration and the company seal. After the permit, the process moves on to the labor contract and the worker's visa; the EPS site, eps.go.kr, describes the current sequence.

Practical checks before you apply

  • Match your industry code to the notice for the round.
  • Count the recruitment days backwards from the first day of the window.
  • Prepare the housing documents and photos in advance.
  • Read the notice's list of documents and its submission method, since regional offices can differ in small details.

FAQ

How long must I recruit Korean workers first?

MOEL's 2026 round notices ask for at least 7 days before you apply. Confirm the figure in the notice for your round.

Can I apply at any time of year?

No. Applications are accepted only in the windows MOEL announces for each round.

Where can I ask questions?

MOEL's contact center is reachable on 1350, and your regional employment and labor office handles applications for your area.
