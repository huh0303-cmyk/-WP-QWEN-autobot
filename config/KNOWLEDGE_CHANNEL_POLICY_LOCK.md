# KNOWLEDGE CHANNEL POLICY LOCK

Locked on 2026-08-30. These rules apply to the five English knowledge channels.

## Shared production rules

- Publish each channel once after a random 2-3 day interval at an irregular KST hour and minute.
- Acquire verified, reusable source footage first; write narration against the acquired shot list second.
- Extract and AI-inspect three real frames (opening/middle/closing) from every candidate clip. Reject clips scoring below 75/100 for visible relevance.
- Write narration only after the footage is locked, against the inspected shot sequence in its actual playback order.
- After writing, run a second continuity review against the inspected clip order. A narration/footage alignment score below 90/100 is a hard failure: do not render or upload even privately.
- Maintain a source ledger containing the original URL, institution, title, date, creator, rights statement and retrieval date.
- Do not use unrelated filler. Every important narration passage must have a semantically matching clip or still.
- Narrated knowledge channels choose one documentary-suitable ElevenLabs voice per production from available American/British male/female voices; blank or unrelated character voices are forbidden.
- Silent Era is wordless. It does not receive explanatory narration.
- Upload PRIVATE for human review. Never auto-publish publicly.

## NASA & Space Times

- Search NASA and other verified public institutions aggressively: NASA Image and Video Library, NASA missions and centers, US Congress/LOC, US government agencies, universities and observatories.
- NASA material is not assumed safe merely because NASA hosts it. Check third-party credits, logos, music and separate restrictions.
- Mission, spacecraft and event narration must track the footage shown. Preserve NASA asset IDs and source URLs.

## History Today Times

- The subject is a genuine event that occurred on the current calendar date.
- Build an engaging story from real archival footage: context, conflict, turning point, result and present-day meaning.
- Thumbnail foreground text is the English date in very large type, for example `AUGUST 30`, with the smaller line `THIS DAY IN HISTORY`.
- Preferred thumbnail imagery includes black-and-white war photography, American prosperity-era life, leaders, streets, inventions, newspapers and official records. Use one strong image or a clean 2-3 image collage.

## Invention Times

- Focus on the problem, failed attempts, inventor, patent or mechanism, improvement and social effect.
- Prefer patent offices, museums, universities, government archives, LOC, Internet Archive and verified institutional collections.
- Editing is comparatively brisk. Narration about a mechanism, person or prototype must show that mechanism, person or prototype.

## Silent Era Times

- Wordless public-domain silent-film presentation with Charlie Chaplin strongly represented alongside Buster Keaton and other verified silent-era works.
- Film clips, photographs and names are used confidently when the specific asset's rights permit it.
- Check each film, photograph, restoration, score and re-edit separately. A modern restoration or soundtrack can carry new rights.
- Do not imply celebrity endorsement and do not add synthetic spoken narration.

## Retro Reels Times

- Evoke American memory through authentic people, facial expressions, household objects, shops, cars, schools, diners, roads, advertisements and daily life.
- Keep it distinct from Silent Era: this channel is social memory and material culture, not a movie-history channel.
