## Audience and buying intent

English-reading foreign residents preparing to work in Korea, international students entering the Korean job market, foreign workers already employed in Korea, and foreign professionals working in Korean offices.

Prioritize products with clear practical use, repeatable everyday need, easy product comparison, and a reasonable chance of purchase after reading the article. Higher-priced products are included where the article's use case supports them; never insert an expensive item merely to increase order value.

## Per-post placement rule

Each eligible published article receives **5–10 products** selected individually from this pool:
- 2–4 products directly related to the article's topic and reader intent.
- 1–3 practical Korea-living / commuting / dormitory essentials where relevant.
- 1–3 higher-priced work, study or digital products where relevant.

Counts and order are flexible. Select only products that make sense for that specific article. Avoid the same product combination across posts and track placement frequency. Legal, visa and rights explainers may receive fewer or no products if there is no natural purchase intent.

## Placement and compliance

- Use the exact portal-generated product link for that exact item. Never reuse a URL or iframe from another product.
- Prefer the portal-provided official creative/tag. Do not use generated or stock images as a substitute for the exact product image.
- Add the required disclosure: `이 포스팅은 쿠팡 파트너스 활동의 일환으로, 이에 따른 일정액의 수수료를 제공받습니다.`
- Add `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` to affiliate links.
- Do not claim best-seller status, sales rank, ratings, reviews or discounts unless directly observed and timestamped.
- For PPE, confirm relevant certification and specifications on the actual product listing.
- Never imply an item is legally required for Korean immigration or employment unless a current official source confirms it.
