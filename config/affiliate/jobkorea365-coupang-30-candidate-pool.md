## Editorial fit

Articles cover lawful work eligibility, visa/work constraints, job applications, Korean resumes and interviews, employment contracts, pay checks, workplace adaptation and practical next steps. Do not promise employment or provide unlawful visa/work advice.

## Per-article default mix
Select 6–7 products per published article:
- 2–3 directly related to the article's topic (resume/interview, job role, work safety, office work, language study)
- 2 Korea-life essentials relevant to the worker's situation (commuting, meals, laundry, dormitory/room, device charging)
- 2 higher-priced items only where the article and reader's likely use justify them (laptop, monitor, work shoes/equipment, tablet)
Randomize among eligible items only after article relevance filtering. Do not force products into unrelated legal/visa explainers.
