## Editorial fit
KStudy365 covers Korean university admissions, eligibility, application schedules, required documents, tuition and scholarships. The reader is a prospective international student comparing Korean university admissions. This catalog must support that journey; it is not a generic student-lifestyle catalog.

## Per-article selection mix (8 cards)
For each eligible article, select up to:
- 2 directly relevant study/admissions products
- 2 currently popular / seasonal / high-demand items that fit the article's reader intent
- 2 premium / higher-ticket products when the article context supports the price
- 2 Korea-arrival / campus essentials useful to an international student
This is a selection recipe, not a requirement to force eight items. Use fewer if relevance or verified affiliate inventory is insufficient.

## Selection and validation rules
- These are product types / candidate slots, not approved product listings. Replace each with one current Coupang product selected inside the authenticated Coupang Partners portal.
- Prefer products with clear specifications, current availability, reliable reviews and sensible price/value. Do not invent popularity or sales rank; capture it only if the portal/product page actually shows it.
- For premium products, compare current options and avoid recommending a high price merely to increase commission.
- Check international compatibility: plug shape, voltage range (100–240V where relevant), USB-C standards, keyboard layout, laptop warranty/OS, baggage dimensions and dormitory restrictions.
- No products that promise admission, TOPIK score improvement, visa approval or guaranteed academic results.
- For each selected item, generate its own partner short URL and official image+text / blog HTML tag from the user's logged-in Coupang Partners portal. Never reuse one product's link or iframe for another item.
- Store title, product ID/item ID, price snapshot (timestamped, optional), partner short URL, official HTML/tag, image source/rights basis, category, relevance tags and verified timestamp.
- Do not use generic Coupang homepage URLs as product links.
- Per article, filter by title/body/category/tags first, then choose 2+2+2+2 from eligible groups with rotation fairness. Do not randomize across unrelated site catalogs.
- Append a clearly disclosed card module to existing published posts only. Preserve all original article content and metadata. Dry-run first, then a 3-post pilot and re-fetch verification.

## Research basis
KStudy365's official editorial playbook defines its scope as Korean university degree admissions, eligibility, application schedules and scholarships. The Study in Korea government guide identifies practical pre-arrival needs including laptop, USB, adaptor, earphones/headset, books, stationery and personal documents. University dormitory guidance also lists bedding, backpack, study lamp, power strip, tumbler and laundry essentials. These sources inform the candidate types; they do not establish product popularity or endorsement.
