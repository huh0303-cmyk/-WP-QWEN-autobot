# SITE EDITORIAL PLAYBOOKS — LOCKED

Status: GitHub source of truth. Scope: WordPress 27, Blogger 27, Tistory 5.

## Resolution order

Every writing job resolves instructions in this order: site playbook → platform overlay → current category → verified keyword/search intent → current-source requirements → deterministic quality gates. A model may not replace a site rule with its own preferred style.

## Rules for every site

### Must do

- Name the reader, decision and immediate question before outlining the article.
- Answer the primary question in the first 120 words; then explain conditions, exceptions, cost/time and next action.
- Use current official or primary sources for medical, legal, tax, finance, visa, public-benefit, schedule and policy claims. Put the effective date beside volatile facts.
- Use concrete nouns, dates, units, eligibility conditions and worked examples. Distinguish facts, estimates and editorial interpretation.
- Match one existing category, write a natural meta description, use descriptive ALT text and finish every section.
- Vary headline syntax, opening pattern, section count and examples. Check recent titles before saving.

### Must not do

- Do not use `Unlock`, `Ultimate/Complete/Comprehensive Guide`, `Discover the Power`, `Everything You Need to Know`, `총정리`, `완벽 가이드` or other mass-produced AI formulas.
- Do not invent quotations, statistics, institutions, application dates, prices, diagnoses, legal outcomes or sources.
- Do not copy, translate line by line, spin, or closely paraphrase another article. A source is evidence, not prose to disguise.
- Do not pad limited facts into a long article, repeat the conclusion, use empty motivational language or write a wall of text.
- Do not diagnose, guarantee approval/returns/benefits, hide material risks, or present sponsored language as neutral advice.
- Do not reuse the same title, outline, examples, FAQ or paragraphs across WordPress, Blogger and Tistory.

## Platform overlays

### WordPress

Authoritative specialist article. Use a direct answer, decision table/checklist when useful, source links, 3–6 meaningful H2 sections, concise FAQ and internal links that genuinely help. Save as review draft except the two approved newsrooms.

### Blogger

Independent companion article, never a WP copy. Choose a narrower long-tail intent, new headline and section order, different examples and FAQ. Use 8–14 short noun labels, a natural search description within Blogger's configured limit, one relevant image maximum and ALT text. Save as draft for approval.

### Tistory

Korean search-first practical article. Lead with the answer and update date; use short paragraphs, mobile-scannable headings and one practical checklist/table only when it improves comprehension. Assign one of the site's 3–4 approved categories, add ALT, and save privately for approval.

### Newsrooms

RSS is a lead only. Verify the event through a primary record and at least one additional reliable source, then report independently. Use 700–1,100 characters for a breaking brief, 1,100–1,500 for a standard report, and 1,500–2,000 for analysis; never pad. Preserve exact official names. Use only item-level licensed/public-domain media, or a clearly illustrative AI visual that cannot be mistaken for documentary evidence.

## Image and ALT lock

Default order is SDXL Lightning → FLUX Schnell → text-only. Generated images must contain no text, pseudo-text, logos, documents, forms, screens, signs or watermarks. ALT describes the visible subject and its editorial relevance; it is not a keyword list. Medical, legal, finance and news imagery must not look like fabricated evidence or an official document.
