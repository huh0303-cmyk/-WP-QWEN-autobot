# WP24 ADSENSE APPROVAL POLICY LOCK

Status: **CANONICAL / LOCKED**
Effective: 2026-09-18
Project: London Project
Owner / final decision maker: Chairman
Primary PM: GPT-5.6 Sol

## 1. Primary objective

The highest-priority objective for the 24 regular WordPress sites other than **k-health365.com** is:

> **Make each site ready for Google AdSense review and approval as quickly as practical, without sacrificing policy compliance or content quality.**

K-Health365 is excluded from this WP24 approval campaign because it is already an AdSense-approved asset.

The two newsrooms are managed separately and are not part of this WP24 approval campaign.

## 2. One site = one clear topic

Each WP24 site must have **one unmistakable primary topic** that matches the site's name, brand and intended search audience.

Do not broaden a site merely to create more posts.

A second adjacent topic/category is allowed only when it is clearly necessary and closely related to the primary topic.

### Category limit
- Preferred: **1 core category**
- Allowed when justified: **2 core categories maximum**
- Forbidden as a permanent content bucket: **Etc / 기타 / Uncategorized / miscellaneous catch-all**

Golden Keywords must stay inside the approved topic/category scope.

## 3. Existing-content restructuring

Every existing public post must be classified before restructuring:

- **KEEP** — on-topic, useful, sufficiently trustworthy.
- **IMPROVE** — on-topic but needs factual, structural, SEO, image or readability repair.
- **MERGE** — materially overlaps another post/search intent; consolidate around the strongest useful URL after checking search evidence.
- **PRIVATE_REVIEW** — materially off-topic, low-value, misleading, broken, AI-remnant-heavy, or not worth repairing.

A lower public post count is acceptable. Do not preserve weak/off-topic content merely to keep the site looking large.

## 4. Search-asset safety rule

Never bulk-hide or delete URLs solely because they are not indexed.

Before making a public URL private or merging it, check when available:
- GSC indexing/search visibility
- impressions/clicks/query evidence
- topic fit
- content quality
- duplication/search-intent overlap

URLs with meaningful search traffic or useful search equity require conservative handling.

## 5. Content-quality cleanup

Priority defects to remove or repair:
- exposed AI instructions, image prompts, placeholders or code fences
- fabricated experience/personas
- unsupported or stale numbers, dates, prices, rules or claims
- thin/repetitive/templated articles
- duplicate or near-duplicate search intent
- irrelevant or broken images
- image/content mismatch and poor ALT text
- broken internal links / 404s
- irrelevant tags/categories
- misleading titles
- off-topic content

Health, finance, legal, visa and other consequential topics require stronger source verification.

## 6. Golden Keyword rule

New publishing is permitted only when the keyword:
1. fits the site's locked primary topic;
2. fits one of its maximum two approved categories;
3. is not materially duplicative of an existing post/search intent;
4. has useful evidence such as site-specific GSC query signals, seasonality, current demand or credible topic relevance;
5. can support a genuinely useful article.

Do not equate GSC impressions with market search volume.

## 7. Images

Images are optional, never a publication hard dependency.

Priority:
1. relevant copyright-safe/free image;
2. approved low-cost generated image only when useful;
3. no image when neither is suitable.

Never delay or fail an otherwise useful article merely because image generation failed.

## 8. AdSense readiness evidence

A site is not marked READY merely because a script ran.

Before READY, verify at minimum:
- primary topic is clear
- category structure is 1 or at most 2
- miscellaneous category has been eliminated or emptied
- public posts are predominantly on-topic and useful
- obvious low-quality/AI remnants are resolved
- About / Contact / Privacy / appropriate disclosure pages are accessible
- navigation works
- sitemap/robots/noindex configuration has no obvious blocking defect
- major broken links/images are resolved
- recent publication is consistent with the locked topic

Google makes the final AdSense approval decision. Internal status must be **READY** or **NOT_READY**, not a fabricated approval probability.

## 9. Execution order

- Restructure and audit WP24 independently, site by site.
- A failure on one site must never block the other 23.
- Sites may be submitted for AdSense as soon as each individual site reaches READY; do not wait for all 24.
- Keep exact before/after counts and evidence for each site.

Required per-site report:
- site/domain
- locked primary topic
- category 1
- category 2 (if justified)
- total posts audited
- KEEP count
- IMPROVE count
- MERGE count
- PRIVATE_REVIEW count
- remaining miscellaneous count
- major defects
- GSC evidence status
- AdSense readiness: READY / NOT_READY

## 10. Change control

This file is the canonical policy lock for the WP24 AdSense approval campaign.

Do not loosen the one-topic / maximum-two-category rule, reintroduce miscellaneous mass publishing, or bulk-delete/hide search assets without explicit owner direction and recorded rationale.
