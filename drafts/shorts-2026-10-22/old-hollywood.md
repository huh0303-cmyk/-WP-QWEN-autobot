Title: How Hollywood Learned to Talk
Cover: SOUND CHANGED THE MOVIES

Narration (ElevenLabs only):
In the late 1920s, Hollywood began a rapid transition from silent films to sound films. One influential milestone was The Jazz Singer, which premiered in October 1927. It was not the first film to use synchronized sound, and most of the feature remained silent, but its commercial success helped accelerate the industry's shift. Studios had to rethink recording, theater equipment, and performance styles. The change did not happen overnight, but within a few years, talking pictures had transformed mainstream filmmaking. A few moments of synchronized dialogue helped make a new moviegoing experience feel inevitable.
