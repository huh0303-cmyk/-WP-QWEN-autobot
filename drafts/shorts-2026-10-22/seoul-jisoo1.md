Title: Three Details to Check Before Choosing a Korea Hotel
Cover: CHECK THESE 3
Voice: ElevenLabs only; retain the existing channel narrator.

Planning a Korea trip? Before choosing a hotel, check three details on the reservation page: the cancellation deadline, the total shown before confirmation, and the check-in time if you arrive late. Then check the actual travel time to the subway or the places you plan to visit. A hotel can look close on a map but still take longer to reach. Save the confirmation and reservation number where you can access them offline. Compare the full terms, not just the photos. Smart trips start with a careful check.
