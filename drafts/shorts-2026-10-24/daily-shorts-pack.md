## 7. Old Hollywood / SILENT_ERA_FILM

**Title:** The Jazz Singer Didn’t Invent Movie Sound — It Changed Hollywood

**Thumbnail:** THE FILM THAT CHANGED THE RULES

**Narration (English):**

Here is a famous Hollywood story with one important correction.

The Jazz Singer premiered in 1927 and became a major commercial breakthrough for synchronized singing and brief spoken dialogue. But it was not the first film to use synchronized sound.

Warner Bros.’ Don Juan, released in 1926, already used synchronized music and sound effects. The Jazz Singer mattered because audiences responded to hearing Al Jolson sing and speak in sync with the picture.

Its success helped convince studios that sound films could transform the business.

So the story is not that one movie invented sound. It helped prove that audiences were ready for a new movie experience.
