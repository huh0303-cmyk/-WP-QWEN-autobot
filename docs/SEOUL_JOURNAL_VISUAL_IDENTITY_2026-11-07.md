# SEOUL JOURNAL — visual identity v1.0
Date: 2026-11-07 KST

## Promise
서울저널 | 대한민국 정론지_정치·시사·스포츠 핫이슈
Signature line: 빠르게 전하고, 사실로 판단한다.

## Brand system
- Wordmark: SJ / 서울저널 / SEOUL JOURNAL; original proprietary graphic, never reuse another broadcaster's branding.
- Deep navy #0B1730; yellow #FFD400; white #FFFFFF; alert red #E63B42.
- Primary type: bold Hangul fonts with valid commercial license. Stroke and subtle shadow preserve legibility.
- Newsroom voice: urgent but fair. Strong hook must be supported by evidence, without fabricated confrontations.
- Format: 1080 x 1920 first frame / Shorts, plus optional landscape derivative (1280x720) as appropriate.

## First-frame templates
A SOLO — one topic-relevant rights-cleared politician photo at 50–60% canvas. One 2–5 word factual hook in yellow; white secondary line; restrained red accent.
B DEBATE — two politicians only if actual dispute/reply is documented. Neutral equal-weight portraits, clear visual separator. Avoid implying an in-person confrontation when none occurred.
C SPEECH — authentic speaking photo of the actual person, one accurately attributed 3–7-word quote, source/date and context.
D KBO — licensed photo of involved player/team + factual game action; never rip broadcaster game footage without permission.
E HOT ISSUE — verified subject image and short question-driven headline, no unrelated faces.

## Placement & accessibility
- Logo upper-left (within safe inset).
- Category chip upper-right: 정치·시사 / 프로야구 / 핫이슈.
- Portrait center-to-upper two-thirds; 2-line headline in lower-middle; retain safe space around bottom Shorts UI.
- 1 or 2 faces, max 3 for justified group coverage. Text high contrast, readable on phone; avoid headline overload.
- Avoid color coding parties as good or bad. Apply same brand system regardless of the politician's affiliation.
- Explicitly mark illustrative graphics, and do not present synthetic politician portraits or hypothetical quotes as real documentary evidence.

## Benchmark requirement
- Only cite an individual competitor **Short** as '500k+ validated' when its exact URL, visible views >=500000, observation date, and video type are recorded. A viral channel's aggregate views or a thumbnail from a news article does not qualify.
- Reference outlets: 매일신문, TV조선, 채널A '김진의 돌직구쇼', plus independently verified category-matched Shorts.
- Analyze the hook, readable typography, palette, real subject placement, pacing, 2-second opening; don't duplicate original copyrighted footage, voice or layouts.
- Sample graphic is a **concept mockup**, not a 500k-view video, not a current political statement, and not rights-cleared final publication artwork.
- Every candidate publication requires an underlying verified story, dated original photo licensing info, preview QA, and exact channel identity/authorization validation.
- Current automated Seoul Journal pipeline still has OAuth blocker and static-cover prototype; this document does not imply publishing or full implementation.
