# Playlist thumbnail benchmark — owner requirement

Every playlist thumbnail must benchmark a real playlist channel with at least
500,000 subscribers. Record the channel, subscriber evidence, inspection date,
and observed visual principles. Never state that a benchmark was checked without
checking its channel and actual thumbnails.

## Verified reference, 2026-09-08

- Cafe Music BGM channel: https://www.youtube.com/@cafemusicbgmchannel/videos
- YouTube channel header observed: 15,000,000 subscribers.
- Thumbnail visually inspected: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6o9i6xLqFZ8
- Observations: immersive riverside cafe scene; deep evening colors contrasted
  with warm candle/fire light; a short, clear centered two-word title; scene fills
  the frame. The channel logo is not part of our design.
- Apply the composition principles to an original scene, not a copied image.
- Cafe Romantic review: original Paris riverside cafe at sunset, two coffee cups,
  amber candle, restrained rose/plum palette; text `Sweet Cafe` only.
- Validate the 16:9 composition at mobile thumbnail size; final thumbnail must be
  at least 1280x720 and within YouTube's upload size limit.

## Current sample provenance

Gemini Pro app generated a preview but its original image download did not
complete through the available browser tooling; Docs export contained no image.
Gemini API rejected the one-image request because prepayment credits were depleted.
The 2026-09-08 review background was therefore generated with Codex's built-in
image-generation tool. Do not label this sample image as Gemini-generated.
Recurring generation remains configured for Gemini app exports; there is no
automatic paid fallback or billing top-up.

The sample uses existing approved music, not newly purchased Lyria songs. Language
checks use local Whisper base and retain confidence/transcript evidence. They do
not verify singer gender, rights ownership, or artistic quality.
