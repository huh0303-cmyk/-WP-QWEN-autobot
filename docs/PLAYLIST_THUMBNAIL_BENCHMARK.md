# Playlist thumbnail benchmark — owner requirement

Every playlist thumbnail must be benchmarked against real, publicly viewable playlist videos with at least 500,000 views each. Prefer multiple high-performing examples from channels with established audiences. Record the video URL, exact view count observed, channel, inspection date, and concrete visual principles (subject, crop, contrast, typography, palette, scene, and mobile readability). Subscriber count alone is not a substitute for video views. Never claim a benchmark was checked without opening the actual video and thumbnail.

- Before upload, inspect the final rendered image and verify the file is new, readable at phone size, and appropriate for that channel's genre.
