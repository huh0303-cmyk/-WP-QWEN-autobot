# Five-brand Meta content workflow

Effective: 2026-11-07 (Asia/Seoul)

## Seoul Jisoo — Instagram + Threads automated editorial workflow

Target destinations:
- Instagram: `@seoul_jisoo`
- Threads: `@seoul_jisoo`

Editorial identity: a Korean-language hot-issues and viral-media account covering politics, public affairs, society, economy, international news, sports, entertainment, internet trends, consumer issues, health, and everyday life. The style is lively, witty, accessible, and entertaining, while remaining fact-first.

### Daily publishing plan
- Prepare 3 distinct topics per day for each platform.
- Randomize scheduled times between 08:00 and 23:00 KST.
- Maintain at least 120 minutes between posts for the same account.
- Do not batch-publish missed posts.
- Instagram and Threads may cover the same underlying story, but copy must be independently written for each platform.

### Topic mix
- Politics, policy, and society: 25%
- Sports: 20%
- Viral and internet trends: 25%
- Entertainment and popular culture: 15%
- Economy, consumer issues, health, and everyday life: 15%

Weights are editorial planning targets, not quotas that override important breaking news. Prefer timely, verified stories and vary the three daily topics.

### Voice and writing rules
- Open with a short, attention-catching hook.
- Use natural, conversational Korean.
- Add wit, humor, and light satire without harassment or personal attacks.
- Explain the essential facts and context simply.
- End with a concise takeaway or a question that invites discussion.
- Avoid clickbait, fake urgency, rage bait, and unsupported certainty.

### Research and verification
1. Collect current topic candidates from reliable news outlets, official announcements, sports governing bodies, and original viral sources.
2. Verify current stories against at least two reliable sources where possible.
3. Recheck dates, numbers, names, teams, scores, and schedules.
4. Separate confirmed facts, allegations or claims, and commentary in political/public-affairs posts.
5. Verify the original source and date for viral content; hold stories whose context or provenance is unclear.
6. Do not publish misinformation, manipulated context, unverified rumors, hate, harassment, defamation, or private personal information.
7. Health information must be evidence-based, include limitations, and never promise diagnosis or treatment outcomes.

### Platform-native formats

**Instagram**
- Prefer a clean image or carousel with a strong first-frame headline.
- Caption structure: hook → 2–4 key facts → why it matters → witty but fair commentary → 3–6 relevant hashtags → source/reference link where available.
- Maximum caption length: 2,200 characters.

**Threads**
- Prefer a concise text-first conversation.
- Structure: conversational opening → essential facts/context → short witty observation → question for readers.
- Maximum text length: 500 characters for the current publisher implementation.
- Do not paste the Instagram caption verbatim.

### Automated production pipeline
1. Trend and news discovery.
2. Source reliability and freshness scoring.
3. Select three distinct daily topics.
4. Fact-check and duplicate detection.
5. Generate separate Instagram and Threads drafts.
6. Review factual, safety, health, and commercial claims.
7. Save drafts to the approved queue.
8. Publish only after the destination identity, write permission, and owner authorization are verified.
9. Save platform post ID, public URL, timestamp, media URL, and status as a publication receipt.

### Commerce and affiliate links
Editorial value comes first. Do not force products into political, sports, entertainment, or viral news. Add a product only when directly relevant, use only the owner's verified tracking URL, and include a clear affiliate disclosure.

### Duplicate prevention
Check recent posts by brand, platform, topic, caption, and image. Do not repeat the same topic within the same day's three-post set. A retry must reconcile an existing publication receipt before creating a new post.

### Current execution gate
Content workflow and editorial policy may be configured, but public publishing remains disabled. Do not enable it or perform a public test until the owner separately authorizes it and exact account identity, platform write scopes, and receipt capture are verified. Credentials, tokens, cookies, and secrets must never be committed to GitHub.
