## Thumbnail / opening frame
- Compose for vertical 9:16. Make the person or relevant scene large and recognizable.
- Use a relevant, genuine photograph or cleared footage as the background; preserve its context.
- For political quote cards: left = public figure/photo plus short source-accurate quote; right/lower = Seoul Journal's own labeled commentary. Keep text concise, high-contrast, and within safe margins.
- Use one short Korean headline (about 3–8 words); no tiny copy or clutter.
- Make each upload's image and headline distinct. Select a compelling but accurate opening/cover frame; use a selectable Shorts cover frame if the current upload interface supports it.
- Do not imply a person said or did something absent from the source.

## Edit structure
1. 0–2s: Start with the real moment, a direct question, or consequence; show the hook immediately.
2. 2–8s: Identify who, what happened, and when; establish the source and use relevant imagery.
3. Middle: Explain one core fact. Add a concise opposing response, missing context, or relevant reaction. Change scene/caption with purpose.
4. Ending: State a takeaway or ask a neutral, concrete question; no fabricated cliffhanger.
5. Let the story determine length. Trim dead air while preserving quote context.

## Voice, footage, accuracy
- Use public figures' voices only as authentic audio from a sourced recording and only when reuse is cleared for the intended purpose. Identify speaker and retain context.
- Never clone or imitate a real politician's voice to make new statements. Commentary must be distinguishable from the quoted audio.
- If cleared original audio is unavailable, use a clearly editorial narrator, not an impersonation.
- Use owned, permissioned, licensed, or otherwise cleared photos/footage. Public-figure status alone does not grant reuse rights.
- Separate verified fact, allegation, opinion, and analysis in captions and narration. Date and source fast-moving claims; include material responses and avoid quote edits that reverse meaning.
- Label generated or materially altered media where platform policy requires it.

## Before publishing
- Verify source, date, identity, exact quote, and relevant response.
- Confirm visual relevance, rights/permissions, attribution, and no misleading crop/edit.
- Preview mobile safe zones, spelling, contrast, opening-frame legibility, and audio sync.
- Ensure the title and cover match what the video actually delivers.
- Track channel-specific performance, including early retention and viewed-vs-swiped-away, as well as views. A breakout is a signal to learn from, not a guarantee.
