# Tistory Review & Approval Master Rule

Locked: 2026-08-30

## Non-negotiable workflow
1. Every Tistory article is created as a complete publication-ready post before approval: final title, full body, headings, spacing/readability, labels/tags/search description as applicable, and all images in their exact intended positions.
2. Review must show the article exactly as it is intended to appear after publication, including the actual hero/featured image and inline images. A title-only or text-only review is not acceptable.
3. Each article gets its own review URL. The reviewer must be able to inspect the complete rendered article from that URL.
4. Each review page/email has a clear approval control. Approval is per article. The approval action must be wired to the actual publishing pipeline; never present a decorative or dead approval button as functional.
5. No article is public before explicit approval. After valid approval, publish only the approved article to its mapped Tistory blog and record the result/status.
6. Review email contains five separate article review links when five articles are awaiting approval, with title, site, language, hero-image preview and status.
7. Images must be relevant to the article, natural, non-misleading, readable on mobile, and include appropriate ALT text. Reject publication when the image does not match the article.
8. Korean Tistory properties use Korean unless their site-specific language rule says otherwise. K-trip365 content is ENGLISH. Do not translate K-trip365 articles into Korean merely because the other review items are Korean.
9. Mobile readability is mandatory: short paragraphs, clear H2/H3 hierarchy, useful lists/tables where appropriate, no dense text wall, no clipped images.
10. The approval page must clearly distinguish REVIEW/PENDING, APPROVED/PUBLISHING, PUBLISHED, and FAILED. A failed publish must never be shown as published.

## Five-item approval package
For each item include: site name, final title, language, full rendered article, actual hero image, all inline images, ALT text, metadata preview, individual review URL, and one functional APPROVE & PUBLISH control.
