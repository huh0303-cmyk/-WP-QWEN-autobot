# Coupang Partners multi-site execution SOP ? standard v4.0

The current portfolio standard is three header buttons (Coupang Partners, Olive Young, Trip.com) and exactly ten Coupang Partners product cards. Agoda is disabled.

Each site has its own product catalog and verified account-generated Coupang links. Never reuse a catalog across unrelated sites. Select ten products for article relevance, click intent, credible price mix and evidence-based popularity. Do not impose fixed category quotas.

Process sites sequentially by the approved rollout list. Start with K-Trip365, verify the complete pilot, then expand. Preserve article content and featured images, use idempotent updates, back up before writing, and verify both platform read-back and public page. Report actual updated/verified counts only.
