# London social and YouTube affiliate placement ? current standard v4.0

The article affiliate standard is: three provider buttons (Coupang Partners, Olive Young, Trip.com) plus ten Coupang Partners product cards. No Agoda placement or mixed-provider product-card allocation.

Social/video platforms do not support the same article-card HTML. Use only platform-supported destinations:
- Coupang Partners: ten unique account-generated product links when the platform placement permits them.
- Olive Young: one account reward-code link only after its attribution URL is verified.
- Trip.com: homepage or the relevant one of Hotels, Flights, Tours/Packages, using destination-specific account-generated tracking URLs.
- Agoda: disabled.

Do not force ten links into captions, bios or pinned comments when platform limits or policies do not support them. Keep separate per-channel catalogs, preserve privacy settings, and require a publication receipt/read-back before reporting success.
