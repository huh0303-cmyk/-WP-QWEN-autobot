# SOS Language Publishing Policy — 100 Lessons

## Scope
Apply to Korean SOS, English SOS, and Japanese SOS across Instagram, Threads, and Facebook. Preserve existing accounts and followers.

## Lesson sequence
- Display lesson numbering in every post: 1강/100강, 2강/100강, through 100강/100강.
- Publish three distinct lessons per brand per day, in morning, afternoon, and evening windows with varied non-round minute values.
- A lesson contains exactly three beginner words or expressions and one three-option quiz (A/B/C); randomize correct-answer position.
- Keep the same lesson number and learning topic across Instagram, Threads, and Facebook, while adapting the caption and format to each platform.
- Advance the lesson number only after verifying successful publication on the intended platforms. If one platform fails, retry only that platform without duplicate posts.
- Avoid repeated vocabulary, expressions, titles, media, and quiz questions; track a per-brand 100-lesson ledger and published URLs.

## Language mix
- Korean SOS: English-first explanations for international learners of Korean.
- English SOS: 50% Korean explanation and 50% English explanation.
- Japanese SOS: 50% Korean explanation and 50% English explanation, with Japanese target vocabulary/expressions.
- Chinese SOS is not assigned a language ratio by this policy pending separate instruction.

## Platform format
- Instagram: visual cards or short video, pronunciation audio when available, quiz.
- Threads: concise conversational three-choice quiz and comment prompt.
- Facebook: three items with meaning, short example and quiz.
- Do not report scheduled or published until platform API/URL confirms it.

## Current operational status
This is the approved content policy; saving it does not itself configure scheduling, connect accounts, or publish content.
