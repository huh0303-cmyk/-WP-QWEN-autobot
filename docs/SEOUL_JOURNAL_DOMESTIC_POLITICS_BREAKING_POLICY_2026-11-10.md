# Seoul Journal domestic politics breaking-news policy

Effective 2026-11-10 KST.

## Coverage

Prioritize fast-moving Korean domestic political stories, especially:

- Lee Jae-myung: prosecution withdrawal disputes, government decisions and responses
- Han Dong-hoon: statements, political reactions and appointment related developments
- Kim Min-seok: public disputes, Shincheonji related claims and verified rebuttals
- Jang Dong-hyuk, Choo Mi-ae, Joo Jin-woo, Lee Jin-sook, Kim Tae-woo, Lee Yong-cheol, ministers, prime ministers and Seo Young-kyo

Use current breaking items from Chosun Ilbo and Hankyoreh as contrasting reporting leads. Verify material claims against the original report, official documents or direct statements, and attribute every allegation.

## Editorial format

The opening must identify the central verified event. Then show the principal statement, the response or rebuttal, and the confirmed facts. Keep party and politician framing neutral. Do not present allegations as facts.

## Images

Use a real, current, source-attributed public photograph of the central political figure when available. Keep the image editorially relevant and avoid generic stock photos, AI likenesses or unrelated scenery. Record the image source and license or public-use basis in the package metadata.

## Homepage presentation

The homepage carousel uses three recent domestic political stories, sliding left. The card-news grid uses a newspaper layout with the image on one side and readable headline, summary, category and source on the other side.
