# Naver search-growth research — 2026-10-01

## Scope and evidence boundary

The owner requested an operating model inspired by Naver blogs with very high daily traffic. No connected analytics or current public evidence was found that verifies a specific benchmark blog at 100,000 daily visitors. That number is therefore not used as a claim, target guarantee, or ranking signal.

A third-party MarketLink report covering 17,000 Naver influencer blogs reported examples around 2,216–27,237 average daily visitors in its 2023-10-01 through 2023-12-31 analysis period. This is historical, category-specific evidence, not proof of current traffic or a guarantee for this portfolio.

Reference: `https://marketlink.co.kr/static/insight/Retail%20Spotlight%20Report_2024년%202월(발행날짜기준)_최종.pdf`, page 35.

## Official search guidance

- Naver DataLab keyword trend supports comparison of topic groups, devices, gender, and age ranges. It is treated as relative interest, not an absolute monthly-search counter: `https://datalab.naver.com/keyword/trendSearch.naver`.
- Naver Search Advisor warns that duplicate content and repeated page titles can reduce search exposure: `https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/seo-basic-intro`.
- Naver Search Advisor describes RSS and sitemap submission as a way to help a crawler discover owned-site URLs; submission does not guarantee exposure: `https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/request-feed`.
- Google states that Indexing API is limited to pages with `JobPosting` or a `BroadcastEvent` embedded in `VideoObject`: `https://developers.google.com/search/apis/indexing-api/v3/quickstart`.

## Public Naver-result observations

Reviewed public results included government policy explainers, KTX guides, airport-bus guides, and practical travel posts. Useful recurring patterns were:

1. Put the exact question in the title and answer it immediately.
2. Separate eligibility, date, amount, documents, method, cancellation, and official link into short sections.
3. Use route- or locality-specific detail rather than a generic nationwide article.
4. Refresh time-sensitive facts from the operator at publication time.
5. Link to two or three genuinely related internal posts, not a large link block.
6. Add an original checklist, comparison, or firsthand explanation instead of paraphrasing the source.

Observed failure patterns that must not be learned or copied:

- unsupported discount, fare, refund, or timetable claims;
- keyword repetition used as filler;
- stale year-specific numbers presented as current;
- redirect or monetization links replacing the official source;
- several near-identical posts targeting the same core query on the same day.

## Portfolio application

- N1 부의정석: national/local benefits, taxes, housing, contracts, and household finance, with the financial risk stated plainly.
- N2 헬스의정석: national screenings, vaccinations, seasonal prevention, and hospital-use guidance from KDCA/NHIS sources; no diagnosis or treatment promise.
- N3 생활의정석: benefits, regional support, station-specific rail information, area-specific airport buses, intercity transport, and seasonal practical information.
- A twelve-month candidate calendar creates durable seasonal coverage. Current Naver signals and primary sources decide what is actually written each day.
- Traffic growth is measured from connected platform evidence. It is never inferred from publication count.
