# London Project Affiliate Standard v6.0 — LOCKED

Effective: 2026-11-05 (Asia/Seoul)
Authority: owner-approved instruction in the 2026-11-05 conversation.
This standard supersedes all earlier affiliate product allocation, mixed-provider, category quota, randomization, and header-order rules.

## 1. Product cards — one provider only

Every eligible blog article receives exactly ten product cards. All ten must be Coupang Partners products with real account-generated Coupang Partners tracking URLs and permitted Coupang product images.

Fixed category composition:
- 5 health functional food / health-wellness products
- 5 K-Beauty products, focused on skincare

No other categories may occupy these ten slots. In particular, no laptops, tablets, travel packages, hotel products, Olive Young products, Trip.com products, Agoda products, or other merchants may appear in the ten-card product area.

Use the destination's own catalog. Keep a stable ten-product set per destination until the weekly review decides to replace selected items. Do not randomize the ten items per article. Product title, image, and destination URL must match. Never fabricate tracking URLs, image URLs, prices, sales volume, popularity, or reviews.

The user described the first category as health functional foods. The system may categorize items from the Coupang product listing, but it must not make unsupported claims that every listing is MFDS-registered or display disease-treatment/prevention claims. Do not add medical efficacy copy; use product-page facts and neutral descriptions.

## 2. Header partner banners — separate from the ten cards

At the top of eligible blog articles, show three branded partner banners in this exact order:
1. Coupang Partners
2. OLIVE YOUNG
3. Trip.com

Each banner must use its own authorized affiliate tracking URL and official/authorized brand asset. These are header links only and are not counted among the ten product cards. Do not add Trip.com category text links underneath unless separately approved.

For Coupang, use the official Coupang Partners logo asset according to the applicable brand guidelines. Do not distort, recolor, or crop it. If the brand terms require the logo itself to be non-clickable, use a separate tracking text link.

## 3. Commission disclosure

Place a clear disclosure with the Coupang product cards. Approved English wording:

> This post contains Coupang Partners affiliate links. If you visit Coupang through one of these links and make a purchase within 24 hours, including a purchase of another eligible product, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Attribution rules, cancellations and returns may affect commission eligibility.

Approved Korean wording:

> 이 게시물에는 쿠팡 파트너스 제휴 링크가 포함되어 있습니다. 이 링크를 통해 쿠팡에 방문한 뒤 24시간 이내에 다른 상품을 구매하더라도 운영정책상 실적으로 인정되는 구매라면 수수료를 제공받을 수 있습니다. 구매 경로의 최종 기여, 주문 취소 및 반품 등에 따라 수수료가 인정되지 않을 수 있습니다.

Do not state that every purchase is guaranteed to generate commission. The 24-hour period and eligible purchase attribution are subject to the current Coupang Partners operating terms.

## 4. Platform placement

The same ten-item Coupang product strategy applies to the control.korea365.org registered content network, including:
- WordPress and other supported blog destinations: article footer product cards, header banners at the top.
- Blogspot/Blogger: article footer product cards, header banners where template placement is supported.
- YouTube: tracked links in video descriptions and pinned comments where supported; do not pretend that ten visual product cards can be embedded in a video description.
- TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads: profile/link-in-bio or post/caption placements only where the platform account and current platform rules support them.

The platform-specific presentation may differ, but the ten underlying product slots remain five health/wellness + five K-Beauty, all Coupang Partners. If a platform does not support a placement or edit API, report it as pending; do not claim it was inserted.

## 5. Weekly product review

Review the destination's ten products once each week. Keep the strongest performers. Replace only products that are unavailable, have broken tracking/image assets, are no longer suitable, or underperform against measured clicks/conversions/confirmed commissions. Do not rotate all ten automatically every week without performance evidence.

Every replacement must be captured from the authenticated Coupang Partners account and pass tracking-link, image, duplicate, and destination checks. Save a dated change log and retain the previous catalog for rollback.

## 6. Rollout gate and safety

- Pilot destination: K-Health365.com only.
- Pilot scope: one published article, initially previewed, then applied only after operator review.
- No other blog, Blogspot, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or Threads destination may be changed until the owner explicitly approves fleet expansion.
- Preserve article body, publication status/date, featured image, SEO metadata, and unrelated affiliate links.
- Replace only the managed affiliate block.
- Never delete posts.
- Require authenticated platform read-back and public page verification before reporting success.
- The control.korea365.org registry is authoritative for the eventual destination inventory. Do not invent destination counts or claim a fleet rollout has occurred.
