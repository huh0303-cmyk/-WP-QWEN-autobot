# Affiliate commerce provider architecture ? current standard v4.0

Only three providers are enabled in the current layout: Coupang Partners, Olive Young Global, and Trip.com.

- Coupang Partners supplies all 10 bottom product cards.
- Olive Young Global appears only as a top provider button.
- Trip.com appears as a top button and three category links: hotels, flights, tours/packages.
- Agoda, Alibaba, Airbnb, and Awin are disabled and excluded from this current standard.

Each destination maintains its own catalog and account-generated links. Product cards require verified Coupang tracking URLs and official permitted images. Preserve article content, featured images and publication metadata. Use idempotent replacement and public read-back verification.
