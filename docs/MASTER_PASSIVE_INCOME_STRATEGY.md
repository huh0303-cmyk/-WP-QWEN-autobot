# Korea365 Passive Income Master Strategy

> Owner strategic directive. This document is the north-star strategy for product, content, SEO, automation, and monetization decisions. Do not replace it with an old site-ranking sheet or a platform-specific publishing plan.

## 1. North Star

Build a stable Korea-focused digital business that converts global audience traffic into applications, leads, bookings, commerce, advertising, and education revenue.

The two primary business pillars are:

1. **Study in Korea — KStudy365.com**
2. **Jobs & Workforce in Korea — JobKorea365.com**

K-health365.com is the proven AdSense health asset and remains a major monetization pillar. Other Korea-focused sites and social channels are traffic feeders or commerce satellites supporting the three core properties.

## 2. Core Business Tree

### A. STUDY — KStudy365.com (primary strategic pillar)

Goal: enable students worldwide, initially with Vietnam as the strongest operating market, to discover and apply to Korean universities through the platform.

Target journeys include:
- Korean language programs / D-4 pathway
- Undergraduate degree admission
- Graduate degree admission
- Transfer / pathway programs where applicable
- Scholarships and TOPIK preparation
- Visa and pre-departure guidance
- University matching and inquiry
- Application lead capture and application-status workflow

Target funnel:
Global/Vietnam student -> TOPIK/social/search content -> KStudy365 -> program/university comparison -> eligibility check -> inquiry/application -> university/partner handoff -> enrollment/revenue.

KStudy365 must evolve from a content blog into an application marketplace/portal. Content exists to acquire qualified prospective students, not merely pageviews.

### B. JOBS — JobKorea365.com (primary strategic pillar)

Goal: connect overseas talent and foreign residents with legitimate Korean employment opportunities and workforce pathways.

Target journeys include:
- Job discovery
- Occupation/industry guides
- Salary and working-condition information
- Resume/interview guidance
- Work-visa information
- Employer/recruiter inquiry and lead capture
- Candidate registration

Target funnel:
Global worker -> search/social/job information -> JobKorea365 -> suitable job/pathway -> candidate lead/profile -> employer/recruiter connection -> placement/service revenue where legally and operationally permitted.

### C. HEALTH — K-health365.com (proven monetization pillar)

Status: currently the WordPress property with confirmed Google AdSense approval.

Role:
- Protect AdSense policy quality and approval status
- Grow Korean-language health search traffic
- Monetize primarily through advertising and carefully selected compliant commerce/affiliate opportunities
- Use health/wellness demand to support legitimate health-product commerce without compromising trust or Google policy compliance

## 3. Traffic & Commerce Satellites

Purpose: attract high-intent Korea visitors and long-stay travelers.

Revenue direction: booking/affiliate/referral revenue plus advertising where approved.

### K-beauty commerce — OliveYoungKorea.com + Kskin365.com

Roles:

Revenue direction: compliant affiliate/commerce links, shopping-channel traffic, and advertising when approved.

Examples:

These sites are not independent vanity projects. Each must have a clear audience, search intent, monetization role, and internal-link destination.

## 6. AdSense Approval Strategy

K-health365 is the reference approved property, but other sites must not be submitted blindly.

Before any new AdSense application, require a pre-approval gate covering at minimum:
- Original, useful, audience-specific content
- No mass-produced thin/duplicate pages
- Clear site purpose and navigation
- About / Contact / Privacy / Terms or other policy-relevant pages as appropriate
- No accidental noindex or robots blocking
- Working mobile layout and HTTPS
- Clean categories and no placeholder/demo content
- No misleading claims or policy-risk content
- Sufficient indexable content and evidence of real site maintenance
- Internal linking and author/editorial trust signals where relevant

Classify every property as:
1. READY TO APPLY
2. FIX BEFORE APPLYING
3. DO NOT APPLY YET

Never describe an AdSense approval as guaranteed. Optimize the property before submission and record rejection reasons if Google declines it.

## 7. Publishing Infrastructure

Desired stable architecture:

Google Sheet MASTER / Control Korea365 app
-> Hostinger VPS scheduler + queues + workers
-> platform-specific publishers
-> WordPress / Blogspot / Tistory / Naver / YouTube / Instagram / Threads / TikTok
-> publication result, URL, timestamp, failure reason
-> Control Room + MASTER reporting

GitHub is primarily for source control, deployment, audit trail, rollback, and emergency workflows. Heavy recurring production should run on the paid Hostinger VPS where practical.

A failure on one platform must not stop other platforms. Failed jobs should be isolated and retryable.

## 8. Portfolio Operating Rule

Do not optimize for the number of sites or number of posts. Optimize for:
- qualified organic traffic
- Google indexation
- AdSense readiness/approval
- student applications
- job/candidate leads
- bookings
- commerce conversions
- YouTube/social monetization
- revenue per visitor and revenue per channel

No new feature or site should outrank stabilization, approval, traffic, conversion, or revenue without a clear business case.

## 10. PM Decision Test

Before approving work, ask:
1. Does it increase qualified traffic?
2. Does it improve Google/platform approval probability?
3. Does it improve conversion or revenue?
4. Does it improve reliability or reduce manual work?

If the answer to all four is no, deprioritize it.
